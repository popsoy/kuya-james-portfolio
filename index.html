About me

Websites that look good.

I'm James Ryan Pepito, an AI automation specialist and WordPress developer based in Cebu, Philippines, with more than nine years of web work and 150+ delivered projects. I spent five years at NaXum (2019–2024), moving from Client Champion to Senior Frontend Developer and VP of Design. There I built the Brandout platform on Laravel, its React Native mobile app, its CRM, and the shopping-cart and enrollment pages.

Since 2024 I've focused on automation for small businesses and agencies: n8n, Make and Zapier workflows, GoHighLevel pipelines and funnels, and AI-personalized email through Instantly. For Cooke Media Ltd I built an n8n system that imported 6,000+ leads from Google Sheets, enriched them with Apollo and Apify, and sent personalized outreach at scale. I work part-time and project-based with clients worldwide, with rates from $10 per hour.

FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers on pricing, tools and how we'd work together.

How much does an n8n automation cost?

I charge from $10 per hour. The total depends on how many apps the workflow connects, how much data it handles and whether it needs AI steps such as lead enrichment or personalized messages. After a short call I send a fixed quote or an hour estimate before any work starts.

Can you connect GoHighLevel to monday.com?

Yes. For Cooke Media Ltd I built n8n automations that move leads and pipeline data from GoHighLevel into monday.com. The same approach works for Google Sheets, Slack, Apollo, Instantly and most tools with an API or webhook.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I work remotely from Cebu with clients abroad, on a part-time or project basis. I communicate in English and schedule calls in your time zone. Past clients include businesses in the US, Canada, Spain, Italy and the Philippines.

Which WordPress page builders do you use?

I build with Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder and WPBakery, and I also work with WooCommerce, Shopify and OpenCart for online stores. I can start from a Figma or PSD design, or improve and maintain a site you already have.

Do you do SEO for AI search (GEO and AEO)?

Yes. Along with standard SEO and Google Search Console tracking, I structure pages so AI search tools such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity can understand and cite them. That covers structured data, clear answer passages, FAQ content and site health fixes.
