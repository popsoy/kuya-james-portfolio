Case studies

Automation Results From Recent Projects

Real builds from my work as an AI automation specialist in the Philippines: the problem, the tools, what I built and what it does now.

Cooke Media Ltd · 2024

Cooke Media: 6,000+ leads enriched and contacted with an n8n workflow

Problem. Cooke Media had thousands of prospects in spreadsheets and no time to research each one or write messages by hand.

What I built. An n8n workflow reads each lead from Google Sheets, fetches the prospect's website, has OpenAI summarize it, writes a personalized message and saves it back to the sheet. Leads were sourced and enriched with Apollo and Apify, and pipeline data syncs from GoHighLevel into monday.com.

Result. 6,000+ leads enriched and contacted automatically, with no manual research or copy-paste per lead.

n8nGoogle SheetsOpenAIApolloApifyGoHighLevelmonday.com
Technical demo · n8n lead enrichment automation

Form to CRM and Slack lead triage with an OpenAI agent

Problem. New form sign-ups arrive as raw text, and someone has to read each one, judge the lead and log it before sales can act.

What I built. A Google Form sends each submission to an n8n webhook through Apps Script. An OpenAI agent analyzes the "About Me" answer, a code step turns its output into clean fields, the enriched lead is added or updated in a CRM sheet, and a Slack message tells the team it's ready.

Result. Every submission is analyzed, logged and announced in Slack automatically, with no one reading the inbox first.

Splash Fiberglass Pool Company · 2026

Splash Fiberglass Pools: a price-selector quiz feeding GoHighLevel automation

Problem. Pool buyers in Maryland and Delaware wanted a price before talking to sales, and the team wanted to know each buyer's preferences before the first call.

What I built. An interactive price-selector quiz on the Elementor site. Visitors pick a pool shape and options and get price suggestions instantly. In GoHighLevel, a workflow branches on their answers, sends a personalized email for the pool model they chose, alerts the sales team and updates the opportunity.

Result. Visitors get a price on the spot, and every quiz answer reaches the sales pipeline in GoHighLevel. Try the live quiz ↗

FAQ

Questions Clients Ask

Quick answers on pricing, tools and how we'd work together.

Do you work with US and UK clients from the Philippines?

Yes. I work remotely from Cebu with clients abroad on a part-time or project basis, and past clients include businesses in the US, Canada, Spain, Italy and the Philippines. I communicate in English, usually reply within a day, and schedule calls in your time zone.

How much does an n8n or GoHighLevel automation cost?

I charge from $10 per hour. The total depends on how many apps the workflow connects, how much data it handles and whether it needs AI steps such as lead enrichment or personalized messages. After a short call I send a fixed quote before any work starts. Typical starting prices are in the pricing table.

n8n, Make or Zapier: which should my business use?

Zapier is the easiest to start with and has the most app connections, but costs climb as task volume grows. Make handles multi-step scenarios visually and is cheaper at volume. n8n is the most flexible: it supports code and AI agents and can be self-hosted, so it suits high-volume or AI-heavy workflows. I recommend one after looking at your process.

Can you fix or take over an existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. I can review an existing GoHighLevel account, fix broken workflows, pipelines and funnels, and connect it to the rest of your tools. For Cooke Media Ltd, for example, I built n8n automations that move leads and pipeline data from GoHighLevel into monday.com.

Do you host n8n, or does it run in my account?

Workflows run in your own n8n account, either n8n Cloud or a self-hosted server, so you own the workflows, credentials and data. If you don't have an account yet, I can help you set one up and build everything inside it.

What do I need to provide before you start?

Access to the tools the workflow touches, such as GoHighLevel, Google Sheets, your CRM or email platform, plus a short description or screen recording of how the process works today. Any message templates or rules you want kept also help. I'll list anything else I need after our first call.

Do you do SEO for AI search (GEO and AEO)?

Yes. Along with standard SEO and Google Search Console tracking, I structure pages so AI search tools such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity can understand and cite them. That covers structured data, clear answer passages, FAQ content and site health fixes.
