What I set up

What a GoHighLevel setup includes

A GoHighLevel setup covers the CRM side of your funnel: where leads enter, which pipeline stage they land in, and what happens next without anyone touching them. I build each part and test it with real submissions before handover.

1

Pipelines and calendars

Pipeline stages that match how you actually sell, calendars your leads can book into, and opportunities that move stage automatically when a lead books, replies or goes quiet.

2

Follow-up workflows

Workflows that send the right email for what a lead asked about, alert your sales team, tag the contact and update the opportunity, so no enquiry waits on someone checking the inbox.

3

Funnels and quiz funnels

Funnels and interactive quizzes that capture qualified leads. A quiz tells you what the buyer wants before the first call, and the answers drive which follow-up they get.

4

GoHighLevel connected to your other tools

When GHL needs to talk to apps it doesn't connect to natively, I link it through n8n: monday.com, Google Sheets, Slack and AI steps such as lead enrichment.

FAQ

GoHighLevel questions clients ask

Quick answers on cost, existing accounts and how we'd work together.

How much does a GoHighLevel setup cost?

A GoHighLevel account setup with pipelines, a calendar, one funnel or quiz and 3–5 follow-up workflows starts from $300 and usually takes 1–2 weeks. Ongoing support starts from $200 a month for 20 hours. After a short call I send a fixed quote before any work starts.

Can you fix or take over an existing GoHighLevel account?

Yes. I can review an existing account, fix broken workflows, pipelines and funnels, and connect it to the rest of your tools. Send a short screen recording of what's not working and I'll tell you what needs changing.

Can GoHighLevel connect to monday.com, Google Sheets or Slack?

Yes, through n8n. For Cooke Media Ltd I built n8n automations that move leads and pipeline data from GoHighLevel into monday.com. The same method sends GHL data to Google Sheets for reporting or posts Slack alerts when an opportunity changes. More on that in my n8n automation service.

Do you work with US and UK clients from the Philippines?

Yes. I work remotely from Cebu with clients abroad, including businesses in the US, Canada, Spain and Italy. I communicate in English, usually reply within a day, and schedule calls in your time zone.

What do I need to give you before you start?

Access to your GoHighLevel account and any tools it should connect to, plus a short description or screen recording of how leads move today. Message templates or rules you want kept also help. I'll list anything else after our first call.
