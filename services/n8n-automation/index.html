n8n workflows I build

An n8n workflow is a chain of steps that starts on a trigger, such as a form submission, a new row in a sheet or a schedule, and passes data between your apps automatically. I build these chains, add AI steps where they save real work, and test them on your actual data before handover.

Lead enrichment

Pull leads from Apollo, Apify or Google Sheets, research each one automatically, and write the results back where your team works.

AI-personalized outreach

Have OpenAI read each prospect's website and draft a message for them, then send it through your email tool at scale. Every message is written for one prospect, not one segment.

Form → CRM → Slack routing

Catch form submissions, let an AI agent judge the lead, log clean fields in your CRM and alert the team in Slack, so nobody reads the inbox first.

Custom integrations

Connect apps that don't talk to each other natively, including GoHighLevel and monday.com. When a no-code step isn't enough, I write custom scripts with Claude Code.

n8n, Make or Zapier: which should you use?

Zapier is the easiest to start with and has the most app connections, but costs climb as task volume grows. Make handles multi-step scenarios visually and is cheaper at volume. n8n is the most flexible: it supports code and AI agents and can be self-hosted, so it suits high-volume or AI-heavy workflows. I build in all three and recommend one after looking at your process.
