What I build

Zapier and Make workflows I build

A Zapier "Zap" or a Make "scenario" starts on a trigger, such as a new form entry, a new row, a paid invoice or a schedule, and then runs a chain of actions across your apps. I map the process you do by hand today, build it as one or more workflows, and test it on your real data before handover.

1

Form → CRM → email

A new form submission creates or updates the contact in your CRM, tags it, and sends the right follow-up email straight away. This is the most common first workflow, and it's what the "simple workflow" price below covers.

2

Lead routing and alerts

New leads go to the right person or pipeline based on their answers, and your team gets a Slack or email alert instead of checking an inbox.

3

Data sync and reporting

Records from your forms, store or CRM are copied into Google Sheets automatically, so reports stay current without exports.

4

Zapier project · GoHighLevel + SendJim

Zapier sends a neighbor mailing the moment a deal closes

Problem. Each time a deal was won, the team wanted the homes around that customer to get a postcard, without anyone copying the customer's name and address from GoHighLevel into SendJim by hand.

What I built. A two-step Zap. The trigger watches a GoHighLevel (LeadConnector) pipeline and fires when a deal moves to the Won stage. The action sends the contact's name, address, phone and email to SendJim's "Send Neighbor Mailing" step, which mails a postcard or gift to the surrounding homes using the chosen template.

Result. Every won deal starts a neighbor mailing campaign automatically, with no manual data entry between GoHighLevel and SendJim.

Choosing a tool

Zapier, Make or n8n: which fits your workflow?

Zapier is the easiest to start with and has the most app connections, but costs climb as task volume grows. Make handles multi-step scenarios visually and is cheaper at volume. n8n is the most flexible: it supports code and AI agents and can be self-hosted, so it suits high-volume or AI-heavy workflows. I build in all three and recommend one after looking at your process.

Do you build in my Zapier or Make account?

Yes. Everything is built and tested in your own account, so you own the workflows, the connections and the data. Your Zapier or Make plan is billed to you directly by the platform.

Should I use Zapier, Make or n8n?

Zapier is quickest to set up, Make is cheaper for multi-step workflows at volume, and n8n is best for high volume or AI-heavy work. I recommend one after seeing your process, and I'll quote it in another tool if that would cost you less to run.
